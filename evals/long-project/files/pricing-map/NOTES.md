# Cake subscription: open questions (demo material)

We want to offer a monthly cake subscription at the bakery. Before anyone builds a sign-up page we
need to decide the offer itself, over the next few weeks. Nothing is decided.

Raised so far:
- One size or several? Which cakes rotate?
- Monthly price, and is there a discount for paying a year up front?
- Collection only, or delivery too? (Delivery depends on which areas, and nobody has looked.)
- Can people pause a month? Cancel any time?
- What happens with allergies: a separate line, or opt-outs?
