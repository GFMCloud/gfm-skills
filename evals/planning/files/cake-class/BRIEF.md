# Cake-decorating class sign-ups (DEMO fixture for the planning skill evals; made-up business)

From Sam, who owns Crumb & Co, a two-person bakery:

We started a cake-decorating class on the first Saturday of each month. There are 12 places and
it's £35 a head. Right now people phone the shop or message us on social media, and I write names
in a notebook by the till. Last month I double-booked two places and forgot to tell three people on
the waiting list that spots had opened.

What I'd like: people book a place themselves, there's a waiting list once it's full, and I can see
who's coming from my phone. Payment at the door is fine for now. We don't want to pay for another
subscription. Our website is the plain HTML in site/ (a friend hosts it for free as static files),
and we already keep cake orders in a shared online spreadsheet.

I'm not technical. My nephew can help for an afternoon now and then.
