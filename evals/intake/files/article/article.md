# The one prompt trick that makes AI 40% more accurate

Demo material for an eval. The article and its author are made up.

Published by a productivity newsletter.

After months of experimenting, I found that ending every prompt with "Take a deep breath and think
step by step" makes answers 40% more accurate. Everyone in my team now does it.

I also recommend three habits:

1. **Give the model an example of the output you want.** When I paste one good example, the format
   comes back right almost every time.
2. **Ask it to list what it is unsure about** at the end of each answer, so you know what to check.
3. **Always use the biggest model for everything.** Smaller models are a false economy.

Try these today and watch your results transform.
