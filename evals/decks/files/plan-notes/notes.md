# Notes for a team talk (demo material for an eval; the team is made up)

Want to propose: daily standup becomes a written async update, with one live meeting on Mondays.

- Standup is 15 minutes, 9:30, 8 people. Often runs to 25.
- Two people are in another time zone and join at 7:30 their time.
- People say they zone out when it's not their part.
- Blockers often come up after standup anyway, in chat.
- Tried async for one week in the summer. It felt quieter; not sure anyone measured anything.
- Worry: we lose the social bit. Monday call could keep that.
- Format idea: three lines in the team channel by 10:00 (done, next, blocked).
- Want a decision at the end of the talk: try it for four weeks?
