# Tidy Notes 4.0 is here

On Tuesday 3 March we're releasing Tidy Notes 4.0, our biggest update yet.

**Work anywhere.** Offline mode is now available on desktop and on your phone, so your notes are
with you on the train, on the plane, wherever.

**Share with your team.** Shared folders let up to 20 people work on the same notes.

**Same price.** Your price doesn't change, and new customers get the same price too.
