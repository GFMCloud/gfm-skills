# Brief: website front page (demo material for the design eval)

Spoke Street Bike Co-op is a volunteer-run workshop where people fix their own bikes with help.

- Who visits: people in the neighbourhood with a broken bike, mostly on their phones, many have never
  fixed a bike.
- What they should do: come to an open workshop, or book a one-to-one repair lesson.
- Open workshops: Tuesday 18:00 to 21:00 and Saturday 10:00 to 14:00. No booking needed.
- Repair lessons: 45 minutes, 15 (pay what you can, nobody turned away). Book by email:
  lessons@spokestreet.example
- Address: 12 Spoke Street, ground floor, step-free entrance.
- Used bikes for sale, refurbished by volunteers: from 60.
- Volunteers wanted: no experience needed.
