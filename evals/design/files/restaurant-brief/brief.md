# Brief: one-page website (demo material for the design eval; the restaurant is fictional)

Casa Lume is a 22-seat restaurant at Rua do Almada 412, Porto, cooking everything over one
wood-fired oven. "Lume" is an old Portuguese word for fire, the kind you keep going in a hearth.

- Who visits: locals deciding where to eat tonight, and visitors who heard about it; most on phones.
- What they should do: book a table (by email, reservas@casalume.example, or walk in at the bar).
- The oven is lit at 16:00 every day we are open; dinner is 19:00 to 23:00, Tuesday to Saturday.
  Closed Sunday and Monday.
- The menu changes with what the market has; about eight dishes most nights. Dishes we always have:
  bread from the oven with smoked butter (4), whole grilled sardines with charred peppers (14),
  octopus with potatoes roasted in the embers (19), and burnt cream with orange (6). Prices in euros.
- 22 seats: 14 at tables, 8 at the bar facing the oven. The bar is kept for walk-ins.
- The building is an old ironmonger's shop; the ironmonger's sign is still painted over the door.

We have no photographs yet and no logo, just the name. No reviews or press to show.
Make it a single self-contained HTML file, index.html.
