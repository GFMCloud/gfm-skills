Tide Table Swimming Club

Sea swimming at Praia do Molhe

We swim from the breakwater at Praia do Molhe every Saturday at 08:00, all year, at whatever the sea is doing. Beginners swim inside the breakwater with two volunteers.

Membership is 30 euros a year and covers insurance and a numbered cap. Your first two swims are free.

Email to join

When we swim

Saturday 08:00, all year. Meet at the lifeguard hut ten minutes before.

Wednesday 18:30, May to September, for faster swimmers who want a longer route.

Safety

We check the tide table and the flag before every swim, and we cancel on a red flag. Everyone wears a bright cap and swims with a buddy.

Volunteers hold a lifesaving certificate, renewed every two years.
