# Brief: product landing page (demo material for the design eval; the product is fictional)

Ferrolho is a bicycle lock made in Braga, Portugal, by a three-person metal workshop.

- The lock is a folding bar lock: six hardened-steel links, 85 cm long when open, 1.2 kg.
- Every lock is cut, riveted and powder-coated in the workshop; about 40 a week.
- Price: 89 euros, posted free within Portugal and Spain. Order by email: encomendas@ferrolho.example
- Colours: graphite, signal red, olive.
- It folds into a holder that bolts under the bottle cage.
- The name: "ferrolho" is the Portuguese word for a door bolt.

Who visits: cyclists who have had a bike stolen, mostly on phones, comparing locks before they buy.
What they should do: order a lock by email.
We have no brand, no logo, no photographs and no reviews yet.
