What a lookup answers

By the end you can say what a DNS lookup asks for and what comes back.

Every step of the move is a change to what lookups return.

The idea

A lookup asks one question: this name, this record type. The answer is a set of records (RFC 1034, section 3.6).

example-bakery.test  A?   →  192.0.2.10

Worked example

Your browser asks for example-bakery.test, type A, and gets the address of the current host.

Practice

What two things does a lookup ask for?

Show answer

A name and a record type.

A records hold addresses.
