How long answers last: TTL

By the end you can read a record's TTL and say how long an old answer can still be used after you change it.

On move day, old answers kept in caches send visitors to the old host.

Warm-up: from memory first

A or CNAME: which one points a name at another name?

Show answer

CNAME.

The idea

Every record carries a TTL, a number of seconds that says how long a resolver may keep the answer (RFC 1035, section 3.2.1).

example-bakery.test  3600  A  192.0.2.10

Worked example

With a TTL of 3600, a visitor's resolver can keep the old address for up to an hour after you change it.
