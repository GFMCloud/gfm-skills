# Committee meeting notes (DEMO)

In today's ever-evolving allotment landscape, the committee came together to delve into the
pivotal issues shaping our vibrant community. It's not just about plots; it's about people,
purpose and possibility.

Key takeaways:

- **Fees:** The committee underscored the importance of keeping rent fair, accessible and
  sustainable, ultimately agreeing to review the fee table before the AGM.
- **Water:** Members highlighted the crucial role of the troughs, a testament to our shared
  commitment to conservation.
- **Events:** The spring plant sale stands as a beacon of community spirit, fostering connection,
  collaboration and growth.

Moving forward, the committee remains steadfast in its dedication to excellence. Next meeting:
second Tuesday of next month.
