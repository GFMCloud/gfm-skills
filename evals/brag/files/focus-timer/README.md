# Tomato Clock

> DEMO PROJECT for the gfm-skills `brag` evals. Made up; not a real product.

A focus timer that refuses to let you skip the break.

- 25 minutes of focus, then a 5 minute break you cannot skip.
- The **Skip break** button runs away from your cursor.
- Counts your streak of honest breaks.

Open `index.html`. Everything is in that one file.
