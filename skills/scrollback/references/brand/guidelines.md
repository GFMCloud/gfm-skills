Scrollback (SB-01) as brand material for design, decks and diagrams. Copy this folder's two files
to a project's `.agents/brand/`. Style preferences only:

- One monospace face, weight 400, no italics; emphasis is a brighter colour, never bold or size.
- Square corners everywhere. No shadows, glows, gradients, icons or emoji.
- The blue is for titles, data marks and focus only: buttons are outlined, never filled.
- One series per chart, no legend; split many series into small charts on a shared scale.
- Dark only on screen. Sentence case; uppercase only for short labels.
