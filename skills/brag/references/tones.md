# Tones

Seven presets. Each sets the energy, the writing voice, the type, the pace and the transitions. A
freeform direction from the user ("museum audio guide", "overproduced mobile game ad") keeps its
own words in the plan and borrows the pacing of the nearest preset.

| Preset | Energy and voice | Type | Pace and cuts | Hook shape | Caption shape |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `default` | Playful, warm, direct; "we" | Mixed case, comfortable weight | 4 to 5 scenes of 3 to 5 s; crossfades, clean slides | A plain observation that sets up the reveal | What it is in the project's own words, then its best line |
| `polished` | Serious, elegant; the product speaks | Light to medium weight, open spacing | 3 to 4 scenes of 4 to 6 s; slow crossfades | The product name or one strong image, full frame | "Introducing X:" plus the site's cleanest line |
| `yc-parody` | Deadpan launch, no winking; the absurdity is the product's | Sentence case, heavy, a monospace for figures | 4 to 5 scenes, one claim each; hard cuts | The problem stated with total seriousness | The problem, solved, with a straight-faced stat from the project |
| `chaotic` | Fast, loud, overexcited | ALL CAPS, heavy, big | 6 to 8 scenes, some under 2 s; flashes, zooms | One shouted claim | Caps claim, an overstated one-liner |
| `deadpan` | Calm, dry, understated | Small, plain, lots of empty space | 3 to 4 long holds; slow fades | One short flat sentence | Two flat sentences: I made X. It does Y. |
| `cinematic` | Trailer scale, big claims | Large display type, tight spacing | 4 to 5 scenes; dramatic wipes | A slow build to the name | The name, then the tagline |
| `app-store` | Smooth, clean, product-tour | Clean sans, medium weight | 4 to 6 feature cards; smooth slides | The name and its one-line promise | "X is live", then three features from the project |

## Choosing when the user didn't say

- A joke product, or one with an absurd premise: `yc-parody` or `default`.
- Something earnest, paid or professional: `polished` or `app-store`.
- A game, a toy, something loud: `chaotic`.
- Something tiny or self-aware: `deadpan`.
- A big launch moment (v1, a rename): `cinematic`.

Say which you chose and why in the plan's `Tone` section, in one line.

## Writing examples (made up, for a demo note-taking app)

- default hook: "Notes apps remember everything. Including what you didn't need."
- polished highlight: "Search that reads your handwriting."
- yc-parody hook: "Every year, millions of sticky notes fall off monitors."
- deadpan outro: "Quillnote. It saves notes."
