Example guidelines (demo). Copy this folder to `local/`, or to `.agents/brand/` in a project, and edit.
asset-review reads this file as style preference for voice, terms and claims.

- Voice: plain and direct; short sentences; no exclamation marks.
- Say "customers", not "clients". Say "Acme Atlas", not "the Atlas platform".
- Claims we never make: "guaranteed savings", "100% uptime", "best-in-class".
- Proof points we may use (with their source): 31% fewer support tickets at a demo retailer,
  12 months, from the annual customer report.
